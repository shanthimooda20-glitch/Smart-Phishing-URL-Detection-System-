How a scan works

Every step below runs locally on your machine.

1
ValidationScheme, length and hostname checks reject anything that is not an analysable http(s) URL.
2
Feature extraction28 lexical and host-based features — length, entropy, subdomain depth, suspicious keywords and more.
3
ClassificationA trained scikit-learn model returns the probability that the URL is phishing.
4
Risk scoring & loggingThe probability becomes a 0–100 risk score, and the scan is stored for the dashboard.
